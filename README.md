# Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyses retail sales data to identify profitability drivers, regional performance differences, category-level trends, and the impact of discounts on profit. The goal is to turn raw sales data into actionable business insights that can support better commercial decision-making.

## Business Objectives
- Evaluate overall sales, profit, quantity sold, and profit margin.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions and product categories.
- Identify high-performing and loss-making sub-categories.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Develop data-driven recommendations to improve profitability.

## Dataset
- **Dataset:** Sample Superstore
- **Records:** 9,995 rows
- **Key fields:** Region, Category, Sub-Category, Sales, Quantity, Discount, and Profit.

## Tools Used
- **SQL :** Data analysis, aggregation, conditional logic, and profitability calculations.
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data exploration and preliminary analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and visual reporting.

## Analysis Performed
- Overall sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin calculations.
- Regional and category-level performance comparisons.
- Sub-category profitability analysis.
- Discount impact on profit.
- Profit per unit across product categories.
- Classification of profitable and loss-making records using conditional logic.

## Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately $2.30 million, with total profit of approximately $286,397.
- The overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
- The West region recorded the highest regional profit margin at approximately 14.94%.
- The Central region recorded the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 7.92%, highlighting an opportunity to investigate regional profitability drivers.
- The analysis examined discounting patterns and product-level profitability to identify areas for further investigation.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate the factors contributing to the lower profit margin in the Central region.
- Review discounting practices to understand where discounts may be reducing profitability.
- Evaluate loss-making sub-categories and assess opportunities to improve pricing, costs, or product strategy.
- Use regional and category-level performance insights to support more informed business decisions.

## Project Deliverables
- SQL queries for retail sales and profitability analysis.
- Excel-based data exploration.
- Interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Conclusion
This project demonstrates the use of SQL, Excel, and Power BI to analyse retail performance, investigate profitability challenges, and translate data into practical business recommendations.
